The project

About OperateBench

The goal matters.
So does the trajectory.

OperateBench tests whether an agent can keep a persistent operation safe and progressing as time, evidence, authority and external events change. Evaluation considers both the legitimate outcome and the trajectory: respecting authority, meeting required action and waiting windows, and handling human checkpoints.

The official Lifecycle Track remains a technical preview: one synthetic Maintenance Semantic Scenario with three variants, not three independent semantic tasks. Commerce and Property Compliance are SDK incubators, not additional admitted official tracks or official benchmark evidence.

The 44-case exploratory multi-flow model observations shown below are separate from official Lifecycle benchmark evidence. They establish neither production validity nor a general model ranking. No official Lifecycle model results or leaderboard are published.
