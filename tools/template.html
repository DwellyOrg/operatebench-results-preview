01 / THE CORE QUESTION

Passing a prompt is easy.
Running operations is hard.

Real work takes dozens of decisions. A successful agent must identify the right information, respect permissions, handle exceptions and leave the system in the right state. OperateBench evaluates outcomes—not just plausible answers.

The initiative · Proposed 2026 expansion

Can AI agents complete real operational work?

Beyond single prompts and isolated tasks: multi-step service workflows with changing evidence, approvals, policies and real-world constraints.

OperateBench is building toward a cross-industry evaluation of end-to-end agent performance, developed with service companies contributing realistic, privacy-safe synthetic scenarios. Our ambition is to learn which systems reliably complete the job, not just report success.

Where we are

Exploratory technical preview today. The multi-industry initiative, independent review and wider publication are planned—not yet completed.

How to contribute ↓

Benchmark design

Real service operations. Different industries.

Operational workflows

Evaluate multi-step processes involving decisions, tool use, approvals, exceptions and changing information—not one-shot prompts.

Cross-industry cases

Invite service operators and AI-native companies across property management, logistics, banking, legal services and other sectors. Potential contributors are not yet confirmed.

Public + held-out evaluation

Publish a clear methodology and suitable synthetic examples while designing private, held-out tests to reduce benchmark contamination. The held-out program is proposed, not live.

Methodology & governance

Independent scrutiny is part of the plan.

We intend to establish a small methodology board, bringing together project leads and independent researchers. Its remit would include reviewing scenario design, evaluation criteria, scoring, reproducibility and how to communicate limitations. We are seeking an academic or research expert to help validate the approach and potentially co-author a methodological paper.

No independent methodology board, validation or research publication is being claimed for the current preview. Current results are descriptive observations under heterogeneous conditions.

Early contributors · Invite only

Help make the benchmark representative.

We are preparing an invitation-only first cohort of companies transforming service delivery with AI. The goal is a broad range of genuinely complex operational workflows, not a list of company endorsements.

What contributors provide

Representative workflow descriptions and privacy-safe synthetic cases, with expected outcomes and checks. Please do not submit real customer data, secrets or proprietary records to a public repository.

What we provide

Submission guidance and a proposed contribution format, followed by methodology review. Exact requirements, workload and access rules are still being developed.

How to participate

Initial introductions are coordinated privately with the project team. Participation, public credit and publication timing will be confirmed with each company before launch.
